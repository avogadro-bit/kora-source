THE PROJECT

Your studio, without a camera.

Compatible RAW files are developed locally (RAF, DNG, CR2/CR3, NEF, ARW, RW2, ORF, and other formats). Settings modify the pixels, and images can be exported as JPEG or 8/16-bit TIFF.

Ten simulations use the official GFX ETERNA 55 LUTs. Every RAW file passes through a common input pipeline before the film look: decoding, file white balance, color conversion, and exposure normalization. Inputs are identified in the photo information. Format support depends on the cameras and compression modes recognized by the decoder. Matches against Fuji JPEGs were measured on a limited set and remain imperfect. Recipe controls are independent approximations.

Kodachrome 64 is a built-in experimental model derived from published Kodak E-88 curves. Its colors and exposure are approximations, not a calibrated reproduction of film. No Fujifilm LUT download is needed for this special film.

Official LUTs · photo adapter active

Fuji proprietary Lens Modulation Optimizer and multi-exposure HDR are unavailable. Digital crop does not use super-resolution. JSON recipes are not native X RAW STUDIO profiles.

 FIRST-RUN SETUP

Install the official film LUTs

The LUT files stay outside this project. Download GFX ETERNA 55 v1.10 directly from Fujifilm, then select the ZIP or its extracted folder. The studio verifies every LUT before installing it.
